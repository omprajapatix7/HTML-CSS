About Breaking Bad

Breaking Bad is an American crime drama television series created by Vince Gilligan.

The story follows Walter White, a chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing and selling methamphetamine after being diagnosed with cancer.

As Walter becomes deeply involved in the criminal world, his life and relationships change dramatically.

The series is known for its strong characters, intense storytelling and dramatic plot.
